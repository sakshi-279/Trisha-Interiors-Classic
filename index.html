Where Modern Architectural Elegance Meets Ancient Vastu Science.

Located in the heart of CIDCO at Kamgar Chowk, Mayanagar Colony, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Trisha Interiors And Designs is an esteemed interior design and space planning firm dedicated to turning everyday houses into architectural havens.
